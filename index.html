What is 7th Dimension?

An exclusive space, built for ARMY.

7TH DIMENSION is an online community created for ARMYs to connect with one another, discuss BTS, and build lasting communities together — away from noise, algorithms, and crowds.

The Dimension

Seven members. One global ARMY.

Named for the seven who started it all, 7TH DIMENSION exists as a single shared space where ARMY from every corner of the world can meet as one community, not scattered across a thousand separate rooms.

Exclusive by design

Membership is reviewed, not automatic.

Every application is read and reviewed by the 7TH DIMENSION admin team before access is granted. This keeps the space intentional, safe, and genuinely built for ARMY.

Communities

Spaces to gather, built by ARMY.

Featured ARMYs can build their own communities around biases, activities, or interests. Approved members discover, join, and grow spaces of their own.

Featured ARMYs

Recognition for standout members.

Featured ARMY status is chosen by the admin team for members whose contributions stand out — and unlocks the ability to build a community.

Safety

Moderated, reviewed, and protected.

Every account passes through application review. Roles, community limits, and access levels are controlled at the database level, not the browser, so no member can grant themselves permissions they weren't given.

01

Reviewed applications

Every new member is read and approved individually before they can access the community.

02

Server-side permissions

Roles, community ownership limits, and member caps are enforced by the database, never by the app running in your browser.

03

Admin oversight

A dedicated admin team reviews applications, moderates communities, and keeps the space aligned with its purpose.
